Questions

Before you order

Cannot find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person replies.

How long do press-on sets last?

With adhesive tabs, a few days to a week. With nail glue and proper prep, two to three weeks is common. Care and nail growth change this.

Where do you deliver?

Everywhere in India. Delhi NCR orders usually arrive in one to two days, other cities in three to six.

Can I reuse a set?

Yes. Soak off the adhesive, clean the nails with alcohol, and press again with fresh tabs.

Which areas do home visits cover?

Delhi, Noida and Gurgaon. Enter your pincode in the booking steps and we check it right away.

What if I need to cancel a visit?

Free up to 24 hours before your slot. After that, message us and we will find another time.

How do I pay?

Online orders use a secure payment page for UPI, cards and netbanking, plus cash on delivery. Home visits are paid after the service.
